## 2. What's working — keep these

- **"The context tax" section** is the strongest marketing asset on the page. The before/after ("Let me remind you — we're a B2B SaaS tool for data teams…" vs. "Context already loaded, with receipts") dramatizes the problem better than any competitor's site does.
- **The DataPulse demo thread** (38% connector drop-off → decision 05-12 → PRD "Setup v2") is consistent across all 13 prototype screens. That narrative coherence reads as product maturity — rare and valuable.
- **The trust line**: "research preview · your brain commits locally · never pushed anywhere you don't control." This is the correct counter-position against Glean/Granola/Notion cloud gravity. It deserves promotion, not footnote placement.
- **"Answers come with receipts: every claim links back to the memory it came from."** This is your one-sentence differentiator. It should be much higher on the page.

## 4. Structural findings (beyond copy)

1. **Zero crawlable content.** The SPA ships one visible line to crawlers (`<title>` only — verified: 775-byte HTML shell, no meta description, no OG/Twitter tags). Google sees almost nothing; a LinkedIn share renders bare. For a personal-branding-led GTM this silently taxes every post you write. Fix when implementing: prerender/SSG (Vite SSG or plain HTML) + OG tags. *(Not fixed now per phase rules.)*
2. **No analytics/capture instrumentation observed** in the bundle — you cannot currently answer kill-test #5 (does content convert?) from this page.
3. **Naming risk is real, not cosmetic:** "PM OS" is simultaneously Aakash Gupta's product name and mySecond's tagline category. You'd spend your scarce distribution teaching the market a name two better-distributed competitors already own. Worth a deliberate decision before the deck (Phase 5) locks the name in.
4. **Strategy dependency:** rewrites #4, #7, #8, #11 assume the Phase-3 recommendation (brain-seeded onboarding, skills-free/memory-paid, loop teaser). If you instead keep H0 as-is, say so and I'll produce an H0-consistent variant — but note the internal contradiction in #6/#11 exists *within* the current page regardless of strategy choice.
