PM OS · Sources

Connect where decisions actually happen.

You do not need a strategy document to start. Most teams either do not have one or have one that went stale months ago. We read the places where intent is actually expressed, propose what we think you have committed to, and you correct us. If you do have a written strategy, connect it and the first pass will be sharper.

01

Sources

All read-only. We never write to your tools, never post, never comment. Connect two and the first ledger is worth reviewing; connect four and it is worth trusting.

Team chat Slack, Discord

Where most commitments are actually made and never written down again. We read the channels you name, usually #product and one leadership channel. This is the single highest-yield source and the reason a strategy document is optional.

Delivery tracker Linear, Jira

Issues, points, states and pull request titles. This is the other half of every finding: what the team actually did, as opposed to what it said it would do.

Customer calls Gong, Granola, Fireflies

Transcripts produce the sharpest finding we can generate: a decision your team made that a customer contradicted the same week. Last three calls is enough to start. This is the connection most companies hesitate on, so decide it early rather than late.

Documents Notion, Confluence · optional

A strategy doc, board deck or roadmap RFC if one exists. Optional by design. It seeds the ledger faster and raises confidence on day one, but nothing here depends on it.

Not ready yet

Connect at least two sources. We need somewhere intent is expressed and somewhere work is recorded, otherwise there is nothing to compare.

02

What happens next

Roughly twenty minutes of your attention in total, once.

  1. 1

    We read, and propose

    Everything connected above is read once, then weekly. We extract anything that reads like a commitment and put it in your ledger as a proposal, each one tagged with where it came from and how confident we are.

  2. 2

    You confirm or correct

    You review the proposals once. Confirm the real ones, fix the ones we got slightly wrong, dismiss the rest. This is the only part that needs you, and a wrong guess costs you one click.

  3. 3

    The report runs against what you confirmed

    Every Monday, measured only against commitments you verified yourself. Nothing unconfirmed is ever used to tell you that you are off track, which is why a finding is hard to argue with.

  4. 4

    Every correction sticks

    When you reply to a finding with "not drift, we changed that on purpose," the commitment updates and that finding never returns. The ledger gets more accurate every week it is used, and that history does not transfer anywhere else.
